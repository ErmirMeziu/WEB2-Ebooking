Siloso Beach Resort - Sentosa

51 Imbiah Walk Sentosa, Sentosa Island, 099538 Singapore, Singapore -Great location - show map

Offering tranquillity and a rustic environment, Siloso Beach Resort is an award-winning eco resort boasting a landscaped spring water pool with slides for children on Sentosa Island. Surrounded by lush greenery, the resort offers selected rooms with South China Sea views.


Siloso Beach Resort is a 5-minute walk from Sentosa Luge and Skyride, *AJ Hackett offering bungy jump and giant swing experiences and the Beach Station. Vivocity and Harbourfront MRT station is three Sentosa Express stops away.


Rooms at the resort are fitted with flat-screen cable TVs and internet access. Tea/coffee makers are provided, with select rooms offering minibars. Safety deposit boxes and hairdryers are included.


Complimentary eco tours are subject to availability. A dip in the hot tub can be enjoyed in the resort itself. The resort offers self-service laundry and drying facilities.


Local dishes can be enjoyed at EAC@SBR located on Level One (Beach level). A variety of local and western cuisine is offered on the menu.


Couples particularly like the location — they rated it 8.5 for a two-person trip.
